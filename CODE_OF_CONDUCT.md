# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone taking part in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions — is
expected to act in good faith and treat others with respect, regardless of
experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Review the change, not the author.
- Assume competence and a reason behind a decision; ask before concluding.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and say why.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption,
publishing someone else's private information, and posting other people's
credentials or data in issues or pull requests.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/staminna). Reports are handled confidentially.
Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban from the project, at the
maintainer's discretion.
